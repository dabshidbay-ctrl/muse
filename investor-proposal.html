REVENUE MODEL

How We Make Money

Multiple revenue streams across property and commerce — each one scalable and proven in similar markets.

🏠

Property Commission

5–10% per successful rental booking. Monthly rentals ($300–$1,500) and short-term stays ($30–$80/night). Processed via Sifalo Pay mobile money.

🏪

Shop Subscription

$29–$99/month per business. Tiered: Basic (profile + listing), Premium (promotion + analytics), Enterprise (multi-location + API).

📢

Featured Listings

Paid placement in search results, home screen banners, and push notification campaigns. $10–$50 per campaign.

🛋️

Marketplace Fees

3–5% transaction fee on furniture and goods sold through the marketplace. High volume, low friction.

📊

Premium Analytics

Advanced sales reports, customer insights, and market trends for business owners. $19/month add-on.

🔗

Delivery Partnerships

Referral fees from delivery/logistics partners when shop orders are fulfilled. 5% referral commission.
